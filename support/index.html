Contact

Email alexrobertson518@gmail.com for support.

Please include your iOS version, DeskBurst app version and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, Apple Account credentials, payment details, health information or other sensitive data.

Purchases and themes

DeskBurst does not require an account. Theme purchases are processed by Apple and are associated with the Apple Account used for the purchase. If an owned theme collection is not showing, open Settings → Visual theme and choose Restore purchases.

Notifications

DeskBurst schedules movement reminders locally on your device. You can review notification permission in iPhone Settings and adjust your schedule inside DeskBurst.

Your data

DeskBurst is local-first and account-free. Read the DeskBurst Privacy Policy for details.
