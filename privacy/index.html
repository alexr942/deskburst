YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

DeskBurst is designed as a local-first, account-free app.

Information stored on your device

DeskBurst stores your work schedule, fitness preferences, exercise selections, notification preferences, daily plans, completion history, milestones, theme selection, verified purchase continuity and limited diagnostic information locally on your device. This information is used to provide the app's features and is not automatically uploaded to DeskBurst or sold to third parties.

Notifications

If you grant permission, DeskBurst schedules notifications locally on your device according to your chosen workday. Notification permission can be changed in iOS Settings. DeskBurst does not operate a remote push-notification service.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. DeskBurst does not receive or store your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase and transaction information under Apple's own privacy policy. DeskBurst stores only the locally verified entitlement information needed to keep purchased themes available and to reconcile them with the App Store.

Sound and device access

DeskBurst plays bundled sound effects and optional foreground soundtracks. It does not request microphone access or record audio.

Sharing and analytics

DeskBurst does not contain third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not create an account or cloud profile. A voluntary Pilot report can only leave the device when the user explicitly opens the system share sheet and chooses a destination.

Data deletion

Because DeskBurst data is held locally, deleting the app removes its locally stored workout data and preferences. App Store purchase records remain associated with the user's Apple Account and may be restored through Apple.

Children

DeskBurst is a general fitness utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when DeskBurst's behaviour changes. Questions can be sent to alexrobertson518@gmail.com.
