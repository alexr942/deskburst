YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

DeskBurst is designed to be local-first and account-free.

Information stored on your device

Your schedule, exercise preferences, completed movement history, achievements, theme selection, accessibility choices and other app settings are stored locally on your device. DeskBurst does not require you to create an account.

Notifications

Movement reminders are scheduled locally using your chosen workday schedule and notification settings. DeskBurst does not operate a remote notification or user-profile service.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. DeskBurst receives only the purchase information needed to unlock or restore purchased content. DeskBurst does not receive or store your card details or Apple Account password.

Sound and microphone

DeskBurst may play theme soundtracks, interface sounds and notification sounds according to your settings. DeskBurst does not use or request microphone access.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

DeskBurst does not include third-party advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking.

Deleting your data

Because DeskBurst data is stored locally, deleting the app removes its locally stored data. Apple may retain purchase history under its own policies so that eligible purchases can be restored.

Children

DeskBurst is a general movement and productivity utility. It is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
